Blocking Foul

A blocking foul is when a defender has an illegal guarding position on the ball carrier, a referee will signal this by placing both hands on their hips.

Charging Foul

If the defensive player is in a legal guarding position and the offensive player knocks them off of their spot, a charging foul is called. A Referee will signal this by doing a pushing motion with one hand on another

Hand Checking Foul

Defensive players are not allowed to limit a ball-handlers ability to move with their hands.If the defender does limit the movement with their hands, a hand-checking foul is called For this signal, the referee will chop one hand across the opposite wrist.

Holding Foul

In basketball, you are not allowed to grab another player by the jersey or any of their body parts If this happens, a holding foul is called, the ref will signal this by holding his left wrist with his right hand
