Rules of Basketball

Rules for Ball Handlers

Travel

Travel violation is when the ball handler jumps without releasing the ball or taking more than two steps without dribbling or releasing the ball a referee will signal this violation by rolling his arms.

Double Dribble

Double Dribble Violation is when the ball is dribbled, held with two hands and dribbled again a referee will signal this violation with a dribbling motion with both hands.

Carry Violation

A carrying violation is called when a player is dribbling the basketball and puts their hand under it, then continues dribbling, a referee signals a carry by using one of their hands and flip it over from palm up to palm down several times.

Backcourt Violation

A Backcourt Violation is where the Ball Carrier crosses half court onto the offensive end and then goes back over that line, a referee will signal this by moving there arm side to side.

Kicking

In basketball, a player may not kick the ball intentionally, a referee will signal this by doing a kicking motion with their leg
